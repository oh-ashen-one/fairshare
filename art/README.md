# Fairshare supporting artwork

Generated with the built-in Codex image-generation tool for the September 15, 2026 redesign. One image requested and selected; no external stock assets. The unchanged generated source is research-lens.png. An optimized WebP copy is used by Remotion to produce the site's poster and animation.

## Final generation prompt

> Create one refined editorial 3D illustration for Fairshare, an open-source class-action settlement research guide. Landscape 3:2 asset on a uniform very pale cool blue background (#eef3ff). A single softly rounded translucent cobalt-blue glass magnifying lens resting diagonally over an ivory paper document with three subtle embossed horizontal lines and a small embossed checkmark. The visual metaphor is careful research and clarity. Sophisticated minimalist product photography, physically plausible glass/refraction, soft daylight, beautiful precise contact shadows, tactile paper, restrained blue and white palette. Objects occupy the center 55 percent with generous quiet negative space. No coins, no money, no banknotes, no scales, no text, no lettering, no logos, no interface, no busy decorations. Crisp polished high-end independent software brand artwork; approachable, calm, trustworthy. Deliver a standalone supporting illustration, not a webpage mockup.

## Conversion

On the Studio, the source was resized to a 1024-pixel-wide WebP using ffmpeg at quality 85. The original is preserved. The public site serves only the rendered JPEG poster and H.264 motion asset; it does not load the full-resolution source image.

The art is illustrative and does not depict a real settlement, verification certificate, or endorsement.
