# Privacy boundaries

Last reviewed: September 15, 2026.

## The public Fairshare website

There is no personal intake form, account, profile database, analytics integration, external font request, advertising script, or application backend. Our code sets no cookies and uses no local/session storage. Copying the prompt writes that public text to your clipboard; nothing reads your clipboard. Downloading it requests a static text file. Ordinary asset requests still reach the host.

GitHub Pages or another host may process IP addresses and standard access/security logs under its own policy. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). A private Sites preview can also require its hosting provider's authentication. Fairshare cannot promise that hosting infrastructure logs nothing. Public GitHub contributions and their metadata are public.

## Your agent and provider

The conversation happens in the agent you choose. A local desktop/terminal program can still use a cloud model. Prompts, files, logs, and tool calls may go to AI, search, browser, or telemetry providers depending on your configuration. Fairshare cannot change or verify those settings for you. A fully local model reduces model-provider exposure but live searches still contact outside services. Device backups and shared folders can also copy local reports.

Use broad product names, dates, and states for discovery. Names, street addresses, identity documents, account numbers, payment details, claim credentials, and unredacted notices are unnecessary for initial searches. Do not paste them into public searches or repository issues. You can skip any question.

## Files and memory

Agents should not save your context without your request. If you choose to save a report, use a private location outside this public checkout. Review it for sensitive details before sharing. Git ignore rules are merely a backstop; they do not protect already-tracked files or prevent cloud backups. Deleting a local report does not delete provider logs, chat history, remote backups, or Git history. Manage those separately with the relevant provider.

## Filing

A real administrator may require identifying information to validate and pay a claim. Independently verify the domain through an authoritative source, read its privacy policy, and enter necessary information yourself. Fairshare and its guide do not collect those fields. Never pay an unsolicited requester to release a refund. The [FTC refund FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/enforcement/recent-ftc-cases-resulting-refunds/refund-programs-frequently-asked-questions) explains how to verify FTC programs; its specific rules should not be generalized to every non-FTC settlement.
