# Prepare a claim without handing over control

This checklist is for explanation and user review. It does not authorize an agent to file, sign, accept terms, or contact anyone.

1. Reopen the independently verified official notice and claim page on the filing date. Confirm the deadline, timezone if supplied, and submission/postmark rules.
2. Match each requirement to a fact the user actually knows. List anything missing. A remembered brand alone is not sufficient.
3. Explain required evidence and allowed alternatives from the notice. Never manufacture documents, receipts, losses, or dates. If no proof is requested, the declaration must still be truthful.
4. Help draft non-sensitive answers using confirmed information. Use `[USER TO COMPLETE ON OFFICIAL SITE]` for identifying details, notice codes, account information, payment choices, and unknown facts.
5. Surface the notice's release, certification, objection, and opt-out information without deciding for the user. Individual legal questions belong with a qualified lawyer.
6. The user opens the official site, reviews its privacy policy, enters sensitive details, checks any certifications, signs, and submits themselves. The agent must not click final actions.
7. Suggest that the user keep their own submission receipt and record date, case name, and official administrator contact details. Do not ask them to paste a claim ID here. A receipt means submitted, not accepted or paid.
8. If asked to track later, clarify where the record should be stored and what may be checked. Do not automatically create reminders, access email, or contact an administrator.

For suspicious requests, stop and use independently verified administrator details. The [FTC warns about refund/recovery scams](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/refund-and-recovery-scams); claims of guaranteed money or unsolicited upfront payment demands warrant scrutiny.
