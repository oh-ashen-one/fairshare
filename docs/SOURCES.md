# Source directory and verification hierarchy

Reviewed September 15, 2026. This is a starting directory, not a live database or a guarantee of coverage. Agents must open current sources in each session. Dead links, access blocks, date conflicts, and missing notices belong in the report.

## Authority for eligibility and deadlines

1. The applicable court order and court-approved long-form notice.
2. Current official administrator pages and updates, corroborated against the court/regulator/notice identity.
3. Official regulator announcements and refund instructions where applicable.

A claim domain should have an independent authoritative link or documentary chain establishing it. TLS/HTTPS or a professional-looking page does not prove authenticity. Do not guess domains.

## U.S. starting points

| Source | Role | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [FTC active refunds](https://www.ftc.gov/enforcement/refunds) | Official federal consumer refund programs and named administrators | These are not all class actions; an active refund program is not necessarily accepting new claims |
| [FTC refund FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/enforcement/recent-ftc-cases-resulting-refunds/refund-programs-frequently-asked-questions) | Official verification and process guidance | Applies to FTC programs, not every settlement |
| [U.S. Department of Labor Workers Owed Wages](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/wow) | Official adjacent resource for recovered unpaid wages | Not a settlement catalogue; may require user-run lookup; automated access was blocked during initial review |
| [ClassAction.org settlements](https://www.classaction.org/settlements) | Third-party discovery leads | Read the linked primary notices; listings may include stale or incomplete information |
| State attorneys general and state/federal courts | Official jurisdiction-specific announcements and records | Find the relevant official government domain; not all documents are openly searchable |
| Named administrator case pages | Operational deadlines, notices, forms, and updates | Verify the case/domain through independent authoritative evidence before directing users to submit data |

News, commercial settlement directories, law-firm announcements, app listings, and social posts are discovery only. Law-firm investigation/sign-up pages are not official settlement claims. Distinguish any attorney-client lead form from an administrator's form.

## Country expansion

Ask jurisdiction first. Outside the U.S., search local courts, consumer regulators, labor agencies, and officially appointed administrators. Verify local class/collective action procedures instead of assuming U.S. rules apply. If you cannot find reliable sources or read their language, say so. Do not claim global coverage from this directory.

## Search worksheet

For each entity, check exact name, old name, parent, relevant product, region, and approximate use/work years. Try settlement/claims/administrator/notice terms and category-specific privacy, breach, fees, wages, and subscriptions as appropriate. Record query scope without identity details. Deduplicate by case, not brand. Stop when sources become repetitive or blocked, record that boundary, and suggest a clear next step instead of implying completeness.
